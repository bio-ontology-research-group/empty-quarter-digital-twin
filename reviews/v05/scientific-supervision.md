# Scientific supervision of the v0.5 manuscript

The revision follows the four connected arguments: deductive biological semantics
and causal mechanisms; primary Rub' al-Khali observations; the largest continuous
sand desert and the actual sampling footprint; independent external transfer.

Reviewed results:
- Primary provenance: 551 samples, 61 sites (59 core plus two auxiliary), 217
  site–campaign visits. All 59 core reference coordinates match the original
  March 2023 field ledger. Five campaigns span March 2023–October 2025.
- Ontological connection: joint capacity resolves nonzero marginal overlap bounds
  in 549/550 mapped samples. An explicitly illustrative resource query changes
  by 7.42-fold under unsupported CO admission; the same-information flat control
  agrees. This is an admission/interpretation benefit, not extra predictive
  information created by an ontology or a measured increase in biological flux.
- Namib: quantitative wet/dry contrasts are available for only two sites, with
  five independent subsamples per condition. Other dry sites remain unquantified.
  Native units and within-site uncertainty are retained. Historic field water
  potentials cannot directly validate bottle activation thresholds.
- Sonoran: corrected instrument-response column verified against authors' code.
  Four plots at one residence are not four independent desert sites. Whole
  campaigns are held out; repeated sensor inputs are aggregated to 16 means.
  Soil-response calibration improves on the mean in three of four campaigns,
  with failure in August. The reference and kinetics-only models perform poorly.
- Temperate: all 77 bottles retained, including negative fitted rates. Entire
  soils are held out for amplitude fitting, but threshold provenance remains
  within this study. Meadow transfer fails relative to both baselines.
- The causal interpretation is conditional on specified structural mechanisms.
  No observational causal identification or empirical local flux calibration
  is claimed. Raw-read access and derived-input reproduction are separated.

Novelty: TwinDryLands is a funded research programme ending in 2030; its page
mentions causal-driver analysis but does not document an implemented ontological
reasoning architecture. The other retrieved desertification preprint is conceptual
and lacks a documented primary-data implementation. The manuscript qualifies
its first claim by the specific implemented combination and 'to our knowledge'.

Journal assessment (29 October 2026): PLOS Computational Biology is a plausible
first target as a Research article in Evolution, Ecology & Behavior or Systems
Biology. Its published criteria require significant biological or methodological
insight, rigorous methods and substantial evidence; a geographic first alone
does not meet that bar. The strongest argument is the reusable connection from
primary molecular evidence through deductive reaction admission to conditional
intervention calculations, evaluated across environmental and measurement
contexts. The current evidence establishes an implemented integration and its
limits; it does not establish a new causal identification theorem or superior
prediction attributable to ontology alone. Reviewers may also question the twin
designation without continuous state assimilation. The manuscript states that
limitation explicitly.

The highest-value additional experiment would measure Rub al-Khali retention
curves and hydrogen uptake under controlled wetting, with predictions frozen
before evaluation. That would constrain the local process layer and test the
parameter dependencies identified here. The existing revision is a retrospective
computational study, with independent external datasets and honest failed
transfer cases; those are distinct from prospective local flux validation.

PLOS requires the data needed to reproduce findings to be publicly available at
publication. The raw-read accession and late-November schedule should remain
explicit during submission; confirm public release before publication. Derived
analysis inputs and code are released separately.
