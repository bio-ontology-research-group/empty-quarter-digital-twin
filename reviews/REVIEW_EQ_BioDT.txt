The manuscript titled "A causal-ontological digital twin of the Rub' al-Khali resolves gas-diffusion trade-offs in hyperarid land management" presents EQ-BioDT, a framework combining a desert observation cube, additive constraints motivated by an ontology, statistical predictors, six intervention simulators, and biodiversity-data exports. The reported dataset comprises 70 mapped sites, 180 site-compartment states, 551 shotgun metagenomes and five campaigns along a 1,043 km transect. Spatially blocked ridge regressions achieve reported Spearman correlations of 0.5626 for group 1l hydrogenase genome equivalents and 0.6052 for Shannon diversity. A six-variable reconciliation experiment reduces RMSE from 0.4592 to 0.4531 GE, while scenario models predict rainfall, planting, brine, thermal, grazing and seismic effects and rank six management portfolios. The potentially useful contribution is the explicit separation of aggregate identities from environmental response assumptions, implemented in an inspectable workflow. The evidence does not yet support the stronger contribution advertised in the title: resolving causal management trade-offs in the field.

I cannot recommend acceptance in the present form. This needs substantial reanalysis and, for the intervention claims, independent empirical evidence. I appreciate that the Discussion acknowledges calibration reuse, prescribed phylum abundances, the diffusivity normalization and the uncoupled portfolio modules. These are substantive limitations, however, and several materially affect the central results. There are also implementation defects that the manuscript does not acknowledge. I read both LaTeX manuscripts, the bibliography, the validation report and the implementation, and inspected the three principal figures. I ran the seven repository tests successfully and independently regenerated all five validation tiers; the resulting report matched the deposited JSON exactly. Reproducibility of these numbers on the author's configured machine is therefore demonstrable, but it also reproduces the errors described below. Code locations refer to files under src/eq_biodt/ unless stated otherwise.

Comments:

1. The compartment join loses all 268 root-associated metagenomes. This is the most immediate problem to fix before interpreting the ecological results. The WGS metadata contain 268 PlantRoot, 166 Deep and 117 Surface samples, but COMPARTMENT_NORMALIZATION in data/build_twin_state_cube.py:56 does not include PlantRoot. All 268 samples therefore receive a missing normalized compartment. They are excluded by the groupby that builds state-level WGS means, and fail the subsequent join to state-level environmental measurements in validation/twin_validator.py:123. In the sample-level validation panel, is_root is zero for every sample. The root samples are still present, but are represented by an all-zero compartment indicator vector and median-filled state covariates. This is not equivalent to having correctly matched root measurements, even if the regression can recognize the omitted category indirectly.

The consequence for the cube is particularly serious: all 60 RootAdjacent states have missing wgs_sample_count and identical median-imputed values for each guild and mobilome variable. The collapsed root boxplot in Fig. 1b and vertical root series in Fig. 1c are imputation artefacts, not evidence of a biological compartment pattern. The cube has WGS matches for only 51 subsurface and 45 surface states. Both Sites 59 and 60 have no matched WGS measurements in any compartment, so their guild baselines are also imputed. Please reconcile the metadata vocabulary against the original sample definitions, reject unmapped labels, validate join cardinality and coverage, and retain explicit observed/imputed flags. Audit the ledger and all other compartment mappings at the same time. Rebuild the cube, every validation tier, all intervention baselines and the figures after this correction. Merely adding a caveat to Fig. 1 would not address the downstream effects.

2. The Shannon cross-validation includes observations that were never held out or predicted. In validation/twin_validator.py:225-232, folds are constructed from the WGS panel and then mapped onto the Shannon cube. Site 56 occurs in the latter but not the former. Its three states consequently have missing folds, satisfy the training condition fold != f in all five iterations, never satisfy fold == f, and retain the zero initialization of sh_oof at line 316. These zeros are nevertheless scored as out-of-fold predictions. The stated absence of site overlap does not establish a valid partition for this second dataset.

I reproduced the reported Shannon metrics with these three unassigned states. As a diagnostic, constructing longitude blocks from all 60 cube sites, with the remaining model settings unchanged, gives rho = 0.5502, R2 = 0.2552 and RMSE = 0.8330, rather than 0.6052, 0.2782 and 0.8201. This is not a proposed final reanalysis, since the compartment and preprocessing problems remain. It demonstrates that the headline result depends on an invalid evaluation path. Please initialize predictions to NaN, count test assignments, assert exactly one prediction per eligible observation, and derive or validate folds for every target population. The WGS panel also includes Sites 62 and 64 with missing primary-site climate joins; their spatial assignment needs actual coordinates rather than sorting missing longitudes into a terminal block.

3. Missing values, preprocessing and the sampling hierarchy need to be handled inside the validation design. Column medians are computed before splitting in validation/twin_validator.py:161-195, including the guild columns that subsequently become targets. The builder likewise computes whole-cube medians and pools state measurements over campaigns before LOCO validation. Thus, although ridge standardization correctly uses training-fold statistics, the complete pipeline is not isolated by fold. Please fit imputation and any data-dependent feature selection within training folds, retain only observed outcomes for scoring, and distinguish retrospective prediction using contemporaneous assays from prediction at an unmeasured site or future campaign. LOCO permits the same sites in training and testing and currently uses pooled campaign measurements; it is not a prospective forecast. A forward-in-time evaluation and a combined site/campaign holdout would test stronger transfer claims.

Missing mobilome measurements are explicitly converted to zero richness at lines 216-217. F13PRr1 is missing both richness outcomes and is scored as a biological zero. F5Dr1 has missing guild estimates, which are replaced by medians, and extremely low mobilome counts. These cases should be adjudicated from assay/QC records, not silently converted into ground truth. On the existing viral predictions, simply excluding the missing outcome from scoring changes Pearson r from 0.5773 to 0.7628 and R2 from 0.2983 to 0.5262; this diagnostic does not repair its participation in training. The manuscript's interpretation that the model ranks "the many low-richness samples" poorly is also unsupported by the panel: the observed viral-richness median is 8,932.5, and only one observed sample has fewer than 100 vOTUs. Show the actual target distribution, sequencing-depth distribution and influence diagnostics.

4. The reported p values do not account for the effective experimental units. The 551 metagenomes include repeated visits and compartments within sites. _regression_metrics applies ordinary Pearson and Spearman tests to pooled sample-level predictions, despite this dependence and the shared fitted models across folds. P values such as 2.64 x 10^-47 should not be interpreted as independent confirmation of generalization. Please provide site-clustered uncertainty intervals, spatial-block sensitivity analyses, fold-specific error distributions, and appropriate blocked permutation tests where a null test is needed. For paired contrasts, resample sites and preserve visits within sites; state whether the reported estimand is a median over visits or over site medians. A point prediction lying within a confidence interval for an empirical median is neither predictive-interval calibration nor an equivalence test.

Useful baselines are a training-mean predictor, compartment-only and climate-only models, a sequencing-depth-only mobilome model, and a flexible environmental model evaluated on the same splits. Report incremental value beyond these baselines and sensitivity to ridge penalties and the 0.01 pseudocount. Positive rank correlations coexist with R2 = -0.0175 for group 1h and -0.1687 for CoxL, which materially limits quantitative use. Any feature-set or penalty selection must be prespecified or nested. The pooled R2 is relative to the pooled observed mean, whereas the per-campaign R2 uses each campaign's mean; the Table 1 wording should distinguish these calculations.

5. Predictive validation of ridge regressions does not validate the intervention equations. The abstract says that "EQ-BioDT predicted" the observed guild and diversity outcomes, and the Results move from those benchmarks to counterfactual conclusions. However, the regressions are fitted inside TwinValidationSuite and their fitted coefficients are not the response functions used by the simulators. The virome simulator, for example, uses hand-specified exponential multipliers, not the validated richness regressions. This distinction should be explicit in the abstract, architecture diagram and validation table.

The statement "the surface-versus-subsurface and aridity contrasts provide the more independent checks" overstates Tier 4. The aridity check at validation/twin_validator.py:645-649 correlates the input cube's observed or imputed GE with precipitation; it does not evaluate any simulated intervention. The root comparison reuses the pooled depletion coefficient and then compares its transformed prediction with that same pooled contrast. The surface comparison largely checks the prescribed factor 0.65 against 1.0: log2(0.65) is approximately -0.6215, close to the reported -0.6151 after pseudocounts. The hydrogenase response in that simulator does not depend on temperature, despite the use of 35 and 58 degrees C in this comparison. These are useful implementation/calibration checks, but they do not identify effects of planting, horizon removal or climate manipulation.

Please define the intervention estimands, causal graph, relevant confounders, time horizon and identification or transport assumptions. Paired root and bulk soils control site and visit, but do not randomize root presence, substrate supply or microsite conditions. A deterministic mechanistic model can encode causal hypotheses, but the intervention interpretation remains conditional on those hypotheses. Reserve independent sites or experiments for testing site-specific effect predictions, and add rainfall time series, gas-flux measurements and controlled interventions. Otherwise describe the results as assumption-dependent scenarios and revise "resolves" in the title.

6. The ontology-to-quantity mapping needs stronger semantics and an implementation that matches the description. Supplementary Note 1 says that the constitutive layer reads the axioms of rubalkhali.owl. In the supplied implementation, CausalOntologicalTwinEngine._build_default_polytope instead constructs constraints directly in Python; ontology_axiom fields are descriptive strings. Neither that engine nor the registry compiles the stated OWL axioms into constraints. Loading identifier mappings from YAML/TSV and offering a measurement SPARQL query does not demonstrate this inference step. Please supply a versioned ontology module containing A1-A5, an explicit mapping from its entities to quantities and units, and a tested constraint compiler, or describe the implementation accurately as manually encoded constraints inspired by an ontology.

The finite-additivity lemma is correct under its assumptions, but those assumptions do not automatically hold for every quantity called a guild abundance. Disjoint enzyme classes can support additive counts of enzyme copies normalized by a common denominator. They do not establish disjoint sets of organisms carrying those enzymes, or an exhaustive partition of all hydrogenases into the three measured subgroups. Distinguish gene copies, organisms, activities and flux contributions, and name the aggregate "sum of the three measured hydrogenase subgroups" unless exhaustiveness is established. The same issue applies to summing hydrogenase and CoxL GE as a number of scavenging organisms. State a separate measure and normalization for each compatible partition. Intact-cell DNA is also not synonymous with viable-cell DNA, and an intact/extracellular partition needs an operational account of DNA in dead but intact cells. The strong sentence "every state of a real soil sample satisfies these constraints" is conditional on these biological and measurement assumptions, not a consequence of OWL semantics alone. The relevant set-theoretic semantics are given in the cited W3C OWL 2 Direct Semantics, section 2.3.1.

7. The feasibility guarantee and its numerical implementation are weaker than claimed. The set in Supplementary Note 1 is generally an unbounded polyhedron, not a polytope: GE and power have no finite upper bounds. The projection existence and error proofs remain valid for a nonempty closed convex set. However, the normalized phylum and DNA totals are constrained only to be at most one, not equal to one. The all-zero state therefore receives OCS = 1, as I confirmed. If these are normalized fractions, add the total-equals-one constraints and update the nonemptiness proof, since zero will no longer be a witness.

More seriously, a state with NaN in all 17 variables also receives OCS = 1 because the comparisons in check_violations do not reject nonfinite values. project returns the nonnegative input when SLSQP fails (models/causal_ontological_twin.py:153), which need not satisfy partitions or upper bounds. The manuscript discloses this fallback but still claims a guarantee. Please reject missing/nonfinite constrained coordinates, expose solver status and residuals, check feasibility after optimization, and raise an explicit failure or use a certified feasible fallback. Test infeasible inputs, solver failure and normalization, not only successful examples. Also distinguish the exact Euclidean projection from the preceding heuristic clipping and 0.96 phylum rescaling. Theorem 2 applies to projection of its specified input, not automatically to the whole preprocessing-and-projection composition. OCS checks these numerical constraints, not arbitrary OWL consistency or ecological validity.

8. The projection experiment needs meaningful reconciliation baselines and a narrower novelty claim. Theorem 2 is a standard property of metric projection onto a closed convex set. In Tier 3, the observed aggregate targets are explicitly constructed as sums of leaf targets, so feasibility of the reference vectors and the resulting joint-error improvement are built into the benchmark. Moreover, the leaf and aggregate regressions deliberately use different covariates and penalties. This is a reasonable illustration of reconciling independently developed models, but does not establish that ontological reasoning supplies predictive information unavailable to simpler methods.

Please compare with nonnegative leaf prediction followed by summation, the same-feature/same-penalty ridge models, constrained multitask regression, and ordinary or covariance-weighted reconciliation. With identical linear ridge operators and untransformed additive targets, the aggregate fit equals the sum of the leaf fits before any nonnegativity correction. Relevant missing work includes Wickramasuriya, Athanasopoulos and Hyndman (2019), Optimal Forecast Reconciliation for Hierarchical and Grouped Time Series Through Trace Minimization, doi:10.1080/01621459.2018.1448825, and Wickramasuriya, Turlach and Hyndman (2020), Optimal non-negative forecast reconciliation, doi:10.1007/s11222-020-09930-0. Their relevance is methodological, not a claim that this dataset is a time series.

Report leaf-specific and aggregate-specific errors, site-clustered uncertainty on the 2.63% MSE reduction, and sensitivity to the hierarchy and measurement error. A reduction in the joint Euclidean error does not guarantee improvement of every coordinate, rank correlation or downstream flux. The empirical check at validation/twin_validator.py:554 tests non-increase within tolerance, not strict improvement for every sample. Finally, intervention states already have their aggregates recomputed before projection, so OCS = 1 in those runs is not an independent test of the ecological response functions.

9. Proposition 2 does not justify the implemented class-level causal effect or its uncertainty. The proof establishes minimum-variance linear unbiased estimation under independent errors, exchangeable sibling deviations and a fixed known variance. The three field estimates are obtained from overlapping samples, so independence of their estimation errors cannot be assumed. In addition, tau_eff is calculated from the observed signs; the resulting weights are data-dependent, and the fixed-weight unbiasedness and variance argument does not carry over automatically. Reducing between-sibling variance when signs disagree requires a statistical justification, not an appeal to agreement. With three siblings, a mixed sign pattern gives kappa = 1/3, not the 0.05 floor described for split signs.

There is also an estimand problem. An inverse-variance average of subgroup log2 fold changes is not the log2 fold change of their summed abundance. For baseline subgroup amounts a_k and subgroup effects beta_k, the latter is log2[(sum_k a_k 2^beta_k)/(sum_k a_k)]. Ontological siblinghood alone does not imply that these biological effects are exchangeable or transportable. Please define the intended parent effect, estimate the joint covariance from paired site data, use a hierarchical model or directly estimate the aggregate contrast, and assess coverage and held-out sibling/site prediction. The current routine plugs three fixed estimates and SEs into a formula; it does not validate transport.

10. Proposition 1 is mathematically sound for a linear allocation model, but its intervals do not bound the implemented nonlinear simulations. Supplementary Note 1 calls [0.0975, 0.1731] the bounds on the Shannon response to 6 mm, whereas the simulator reports a Shannon change of 0.3367. The main text qualifies the interval as an "allocation-dependent component", but the implementation does not define or add a separate common component that reconciles these quantities. Drizzle/downpour allocation does not enter the moisture trajectory at all. The interval is computed alongside it from unrelated fixed sensitivities.

Please either implement intervention versions in the response model and propagate their admissible allocations through that model, or identify the linear surrogate explicitly and restrict the claim to it. Establish sources or sensitivity ranges for both beta and minimum shares. These are identification bounds conditional on coefficients, not confidence or prediction intervals. The seismic version is additionally dimensionally invalid: target_parent_total is max(1, line_density + initial_TPH), adding km per square kilometre to grams per kilogram and returning a nonzero interval for a zero/zero input. Use a well-defined scalar intervention quantity or a multidimensional feasible set. Validate nonnegative shares, sum of lower bounds <= 1, and other feasibility conditions instead of silently truncating an invalid remaining share.

11. Relative genomic capacity cannot be converted into volumetric gas power without accounting for biomass and activity. The model scales power directly with gene copies per genome equivalent, while the revegetation narrative says that DNA biomass increases approximately tenfold. A lower fraction of genomes carrying a marker need not imply fewer marker copies per soil volume. Under the strong simplifying assumption that DNA yield tracks microbial genome density comparably in both compartments, 10.3 times the biomass and 0.207 times the relative capacity would give approximately 2.13 times the absolute marker abundance, not a 79.3% absolute loss. This is an illustration of the missing denominator, not an estimate of the true flux.

Please distinguish marker copies per genome, genomes or viable cells per soil mass, enzyme expression, catalytic activity and gas supply. Measure or model the links between them before making claims about primary production. DNA extract concentration also requires extraction-volume and soil-mass normalization before being treated as biomass. The mosaic calculation currently area-averages GE despite unequal biomass; specify whether this represents an average of local ratios or the ratio in a pooled community, and use biomass weighting for the latter. A mechanistic comparison should include absolute gene abundance, trace-gas uptake and carbon-fixation measurements. The cited Bay et al. study combines metagenomics with biogeochemical measurements; it does not supply a universal conversion from this transect's relative GE to power.

12. The gas-diffusion implementation can reverse the compaction effect, and its physical calibration is incomplete. In models/moisture_pulse_diffusion.py:64-66, both the actual and reference Millington-Quirk factors use the same intervened porosity. At baseline water content their ratio is exactly one for every porosity. With theta = 0.018, GE_1l = GE_cox = 1 and the default temperature, I obtain a Millington-Quirk factor of 0.252789 at porosity 0.40 and 0.099922 at porosity 0.22, but calculated H2 power increases from 25.0793 to 27.2742 microW m^-3 because the moisture gate increases. This is a concrete defect in the proposed compaction mechanism, not merely an opportunity to "sharpen" predictions.

Please use absolute effective diffusivity or normalize to a fixed reference state, derive the supply-to-power calculation with dimensions and boundary conditions, and rerun grazing, seismic and portfolio results. Free-air diffusivities, atmospheric concentrations and reaction energies are declared in SoilPhysicalParams but are not used in the power calculation. There is no gas concentration gradient or explicit supply cap. Moreover, the revegetation, brine and refugium modules bypass the diffusion function and use different direct GE-to-power equations. A shared physical module is needed before outputs from these modules can be compared as the same quantity.

For rainfall, verify water balance, drainage and evaporation against measurements, assess depth/porosity sensitivity, and integrate more finely immediately after a pulse. The trajectory labels day 0 as baseline and samples the first wet state only at day 1, so the quoted minimum is a daily sampled minimum, not necessarily the physical minimum. Report integrated energy and time below a biologically justified threshold as well as an extreme value. The code also labels 10 and 11 mm pulses as preserving >65% power even though its own outputs retain only 52.46% and 48.35% under default conditions. Derive advisories from outputs and uncertainty rather than fixed pulse categories. Finally, this is a soil response to prescribed rainfall, not a model of the rainfall increment caused by cloud seeding; keep that distinction explicit.

13. The metabolic model is a small resource-allocation LP with insufficient mass and redox accounting for the claimed mechanistic interpretation. models/metabolic_community_engine.py defines ten pathway variables and four inequalities, with no balanced reaction network, internal-metabolite steady-state equations, electron balance or oxygen constraint. Reduced models can be useful, but their conservation laws still need to be established. Sulfate supply is given a positive ATP contribution through sulfur_redox_flux without specifying a reduced sulfur substrate or a coupled electron donor. In a direct check with H2, CO, DOC, external fixed nitrogen and betaine all zero, sulfate bound 1 alone supports autotrophic carbon fixation 0.30, N2 fixation 0.045 and nitrification 0.045. What oxidation reaction supplies the reducing power for this result? As presently represented, sulfate availability is functioning as a free energy source.

Maintenance is also capped at 92% of estimated available ATP, so the model reduces the survival requirement whenever the environment cannot meet it. This prevents a meaningful test of starvation or energetic infeasibility. The salinity audit checks atpm_required_unmitigated, a prescribed monotone expression, rather than realized maintenance sufficiency, viability or growth. The closed-exchange check is necessary but does not detect the sulfate-only problem. LP failure returns zero fluxes, which could also pass a zero-growth audit unless status is checked.

Please provide the reduced reaction stoichiometry and derivation from any genome-scale models, enforce elemental/electron balance and substrate allocation, calibrate units, and distinguish infeasibility, death and dormancy. Report the objective weights and test alternative objectives and flux variability. Keep required maintenance separate from attainable maintenance and report the deficit rather than silently relaxing the requirement. Add donor-specific closure tests, ATP-generation-cycle tests and independent growth/flux validation. Orth, Thiele and Palsson (2010), What is flux balance analysis?, doi:10.1038/nbt.1614, is an appropriate methodological reference. Calling fluxes "model units" does not make a precise 63.1% ecological benefit empirically established.

14. The provenance and uncertainty of the mechanistic coefficients are missing. The Methods state that "the code repository documents every coefficient and default" and that coefficients come from companion analyses. In practice, the modules contain many numerical literals with comments naming local companion directories. This is not a traceable estimation record. Examples include the 29.8 and 1.95 GE-to-power factors; 0.28 Shannon response; porosity losses per animal; fixed thermal offsets; 82% nodal impact mitigation; hydrocarbon degradation constants; and the dose-dependent increases in CBB and nitrogen-fixation capacities. The current bibliography does not identify the companion analyses that purportedly establish these values.

Please supply a parameter table containing units, admissible range, source dataset or equation, fitting procedure, uncertainty, and whether the value is measured, fitted, transferred or assumed. Dimensionless inoculation doses require an operational meaning and evidence of establishment, persistence and dose-response. Possession of rbcL/hyaB does not itself establish enhanced nitrogen fixation, and a donor plasmid's annotation does not establish its functional expression or transfer in a desert community. Distinguish assumptions about the whole inoculated consortium from functions attributed to the plasmid. Propagate parameter, baseline and structural uncertainty into scenario outcomes and rankings, with global sensitivity analysis. The four-decimal outputs currently convey numerical precision far beyond the evidence. As it stands, the magnitude of the proposed inoculation benefit is speculation.

15. The brine comparison does not isolate ionic composition, and some reported interpretations contradict the code. The statement "Calcium sulfate buffering and sulfate supply therefore keep the sulfur cycle and autotrophy running at salinities at which sodium chloride collapses the community" is not supported by comparing Site 60 at 45 mS/cm, ratio 2.4 and ect/kdp dose 0.8 against Site 59 at 180 mS/cm, ratio 0.15 and ect/kdp dose zero. Site, conductivity, composition and inoculation all change. The code also classifies the 45 mS/cm example itself as NACL_HYPERSALINE_SULFUR_SINK because its sulfate-flux regime requires EC <= 25. Its warning then describes a collapse that the manuscript presents as successful buffering.

Please perform a same-baseline factorial comparison of conductivity, ionic composition and inoculum, and quantify interactions and uncertainty. Specify whether the ionic ratio is molar, mass-based or an equivalent-charge ratio, and model actual salt concentrations and water activity where needed. An EC value is not a conserved additive salt amount. The reported Na-Cl case described as having "no plasmid" still receives the default allorhizobium_rbcl_hyab_dose = 0.5; set every intended inoculum explicitly and regenerate the result. The 77.59% Halobacteriota result is phylum-level dominance, not a monoculture. Neither that fraction nor the imposed minimum Shannon value of 1.35 establishes species-level collapse. Report the prescribed response curves and show how they were validated against observations rather than inferred from two different habitats.

16. The virome model and biosafety decision are not statistically validated quantities. SpatialJSDMViromeModel contains no fitted joint species distribution, residual species covariance or estimated spatial process. Its host coefficient 0.254 is a Mantel correlation used directly as an exponential slope per unit Shannon diversity; a correlation between distance matrices does not identify that response coefficient. Its multiplication of already site-specific baseline richness by absolute host/climate/compartment factors also changes richness under a nominal null intervention. At Site 30, zero cover and zero inocula change viral richness from 7,958 to 10,441.07 and plasmid richness from 195,997 to 271,225.99. The 445,707-to-557,141 plasmid comparison in Table 2 is between two simulated planted cases, not between the observed Site 30 baseline and an intervention.

Please either fit and validate a richness response model, or identify these functions as illustrative assumptions and anchor intervention effects to the baseline covariates. Richness and TPM need a sampling/detection model and a defined catalogue. The ARG risk threshold of 0.25 has no demonstrated calibration to an observed adverse endpoint. For a native surface state with manure load zero and plasmid dose anywhere in [0,1], its formula only ranges from 0.035 to 0.050, so every such inoculation necessarily passes. This is not evidence of biosafety. Rename it an assumed screening score, remove categorical safety claims, and validate against relevant ARG, mobility, host and exposure measurements before drawing deployment conclusions.

17. The portfolio results are a ranking of six prescribed scenarios, not a coupled intervention optimization. The manuscript acknowledges that each simulator starts from the same baseline. Consequently, the mean of six Shannon predictions does not describe the biodiversity under their joint application, and adding revegetation and brine carbon-fixation fluxes can count the same baseline production twice. The objectives also combine a post-rainfall extreme, a diversity peak and a 90-day remediation outcome without a shared time horizon. Rainfall does not interact with compacted porosity, irrigation does not alter the pulse water balance, and grazing-induced vegetation does not alter the planted mosaic. Sequentially chaining the current functions would not by itself solve these problems because several overwrite states or apply absolute baseline factors.

Please formulate shared state variables, time steps and conservation equations, or present this strictly as a decision-score demonstration. Define objectives as changes from an explicit common baseline over a specified area and period. The "do-nothing" candidate includes discharge, planting, grazing and seismic activity, and the portfolio caller omits the Allorhizobium setting so that every brine module receives dose 0.5, including this baseline. The system needs an actual no-action control, explicit defaults and complete provenance.

There are four nondominated candidates, P1-P4. P4 has higher J1 and J2 than the recommended P3, while P1 has lower J4. Thus the sentence "The portfolios that perform best on all four objectives" is incorrect: the recommendation depends on the chosen utility. Report weight and coefficient sensitivity, candidate-set sensitivity and rank uncertainty. Normalization over the six candidates means that adding an extreme candidate can change ranking. The code uses a weighted sum plus 0.15 times a weighted maximum, not a call to DESDEO; state the exact scalarization and avoid implying that DESDEO performed a search. Also justify treating Halobacteriota abundance itself as environmental damage rather than a context-dependent property of a naturally saline ecosystem.

18. The synthetic benchmark supports a narrower claim than the manuscript makes. Validation and the synthetic benchmark use a 60% identity floor, whereas the intervention cube uses 50%. The observed median biases of 0.5959-0.8410 log2 units correspond to substantial absolute overestimation, which matters when GE is converted directly into power. Cancellation in a contrast requires matched multiplicative bias across the two conditions; high correlations and a median bias do not establish that condition for different taxa, depths and sequencing coverages. Eight synthetic root/bulk pairs are insufficient to characterize broad ecological transfer without describing their construction.

Please describe the mock communities, sequencing simulation, abundance ranges, novelty relative to the reference database, zero/low-abundance cases and filtering that yields n = 17-19 per guild. Report absolute error, calibration slope, bias variation and uncertainty at both identity thresholds. Add taxonomically withheld references and sequencing-depth sensitivity where relevant, and carry read-to-guild uncertainty into the cube. Tier 5 currently reads precomputed benchmark summaries, rather than regenerating the read-to-guild evaluation; publish the workflow and inputs needed for an independent rerun. A synthetic rank-recovery result does not validate gene expression, viability or realized flux.

19. The state-cube description, standards exports and biological evidence need to be reconciled. The deposited and rebuilt cubes both contain 60 states in each compartment at 60 primary sites, not 68 surface, 65 subsurface and 47 root states. The ten auxiliary sites occur in the site table; the API borrows a nearest-primary baseline for their simulations. They do not add ten empirically populated compartment profiles. The exported site table's mean annual precipitation ranges from 14.264 to 48.0727 mm and mean annual temperature from 25.8976 to 30.0675 degrees C, rather than the 26-90 mm and 26-29 degrees C in the Results. Please generate these descriptive statistics and figure legends from the same release used for inference.

The taxonomic abundances are explicitly prescribed priors, but the Darwin Core export records them as positive MaterialSample occurrences without per-record evidence status. Median-imputed guild abundances similarly become occurrences. This loses a scientifically essential distinction between observation and model output. The B-Cubed exporter assigns specimen counts to every taxon/guild as occurrence counts, defaulting to three when absent, and estimates richness from Shannon before calculating Pielou evenness. For most rows, H'/log[exp(1.18 H')] is simply 1/1.18, approximately 0.8475; this is not independently measured evenness. Use observed richness and taxon-specific evidence, or mark these values as derived assumptions.

Run actual archive/profile validators and demonstrate import into a receiving biodiversity workflow. File existence tests are not standards validation. Check the Event parent records, which are referenced but not emitted; preserve actual campaign/time identifiers instead of a fixed 2022-2025 interval; distinguish a compartment class from a sampling protocol; and correct semantic roles in eMoF. For example, an element IRI identifies an analyte, not the numerical concentration value; a millimetre unit does not by itself encode millimetres per year; and a quality class is not a GE unit. These issues affect interoperability, not just presentation.

20. The reproducibility and provenance claims exceed what the run records contain. The Discussion states that "A regulator or operator can therefore retrieve the exact input state, parameter settings and model version behind a recommendation, rerun it, and verify that its outputs satisfy the constitutive axioms." FDOProvenanceRecorder hashes the intervention name, site IRI, parameters and output summary. It does not include the exact input state, compartment, source-table hashes, ontology release, source commit, parameter-set version or environment digest. The source-code path used as the instrument is not a model version. Each simulator constructs a crate but returns only fdo_kernel_metadata; automatic persistence of every full run crate is not implemented. The optional CLI --save-crate reconstructs a crate from user-supplied parameters, omitting resolved defaults and the original ambiguity interval. A generated HTTPS identifier is not evidence of deposition or retrieval.

Please return and persist the complete validated crate, including all resolved inputs, model and ontology versions, checksums, solver status, outputs and execution timing. Ensure that the hash covers the intended reproducibility object, and test a round trip that retrieves it and reruns the calculation without the author's environment. The source loaders use absolute paths to several sibling repositories and unpublished companion analyses. uv.lock helps reproduce dependencies but cannot supply those inputs. Package a frozen, licensed analysis release with configurable paths and all required small tables, complete accessions and archive DOIs, and a single command that regenerates the reported results in a clean environment. paper/verify_manuscript_numbers.py prints values but does not assert agreement with the manuscript, which explains why the count and climate discrepancies survive. Add meaningful assertions for data coverage, fold coverage, physical conservation, null interventions and provenance completeness rather than only thresholds on favorable metrics.

The reported wildlife case sets camel density to zero, whereas the code default is 1.5; the camel-only example also requires oryx and gazelle to be zero. - Supplementary Table S3 and the Methods should list all shared defaults, especially the Allorhizobium dose, shade, surface temperature, axle passes and simulation horizon. Distinguish millimolar concentration, millimetres of rain and dimensionless dose consistently.
- Figure 2c's legend obscures the lower simulated contrast. Move it outside the plotting region. Figure 2a should show uncertainty and the weak/negative-R2 targets, not only selected favorable outcomes; Fig. 2b combines dimensionless OCS with GE-valued errors on one axis. Separate these metrics or make the scale distinction unmistakable.
- Figure 1 labels use log2(GE + 10^-4), whereas validation uses log2(GE + 0.01). State both transformations and justify their different pseudocounts. The root-column artefact must be corrected before this figure is interpreted.
- The two-pool DNA implementation uses an imposed viable fraction and its complement, not separate measured pool amounts with identifiable turnover rates. Use terminology that reflects what is computed, and distinguish Shannon diversity from richness throughout the pulse-model description.
- Correct the Carini reference metadata: the publisher citation is Nature Microbiology 2, 16242 (2017), with online publication in December 2016, rather than volume 1, issue 3. Also supply citable versions of the companion causal-ontology and transect analyses on which the methods depend; local directory names are not references.
- Reduce unsupported significant figures in ecological scenario claims after quantifying uncertainty. Replace "monoculture" with the appropriate phylum-dominance description and avoid "biosafety pass", "optimal" and "thermodynamic audit" where only a prescribed score or algebraic invariant has been checked.

I would encourage the authors to focus a revision on a falsifiable question: after repairing the data pipeline, does constraint-based reconciliation improve independent predictions beyond standard reconciliation baselines, and does a calibrated moisture-gas model predict independently measured pulse responses? A reproducible demonstration of those two points would be substantially stronger than six elaborate management recommendations supported mainly by prescribed coefficients.
