EQ-BioDT v0.6: a causal–ontological digital twin of the Rub’ al-Khali

The twin links primary soil-metagenome evidence, OWL deduction of which genomes can carry which functions, a community factor that conditions the soil process model, and paired do() regimes that change only physical inputs. Follow the pipeline from left to right, or jump to a stage.

Scenarios are conditional process calculations with supplied kinetics and soil properties; the metagenomes establish encoded capacity, not activity. Net carbon removal and species-specific growth are not available from this release. Interventional quantities are model-derived, not identified from the observational metagenomes.

OWL deduction: what the reasoner entails about a genome

The Kobayashi-MaRust reasoner classifies all 5,438 reference MAGs in OWL 2 against the full KEGG M00165 module (with its alternative regeneration routes) and hydrogenase guild axioms. The console never downloads all MAGs: search, or list the candidates, and request one genome’s entailments at a time.

    Resource ceiling for the selected MAG

    The ceiling is the largest steady carbohydrate-carbon synthesis rate that element balance, the supplied substrate fluxes and an energy budget (captured fraction of H₂/CO oxidation energy minus maintenance) can support, found by linear programming over three reactions: H₂ oxidation, CO oxidation and CH₂O synthesis. It is an upper bound, not a prediction. It is computed only when the reasoner entails rak:H2AutotrophicCandidateMAG for this genome; CO oxidation is admitted only if rak:COCarrierMAG is also entailed. Otherwise the gate abstains, which is not a claim of biological absence.

    Community factor γ: how deduction enters the process model

    For the active sample, the share of mapped reads from genomes in each entailed class gives qs(C). The community factor γ multiplies the gas-specific reaction coefficient: reference leaves it at 1, gate sets it to 1 if any carrier is present and 0 otherwise, and proportional scales it by the share relative to the median sample (an assumption, not a validated law). Choosing a community conditions the twin; it is not an intervention.

    Mechanisms: the causal graph, admissible targets and treatment versions

    The graph has one node per quantity in a single time slice. Only manipulable inputs and the physical states W (water) and N (salt) can be clamped; ontology, evidence, deduced memberships, constitutive definitions and the activation and uptake mechanisms cannot.

    Version relevance: do(ψ) is ambiguous

    Total water potential ψ = ψm + ψo is constituted, so “set ψ” can be realised by changing water (matric version, handle W) or salt (osmotic version, handle N). Activation depends on ψ alone, so its effect is version-independent. Uptake also depends on the diffusivity of the gas, which depends on water content θ, so water has a path θ → D → J that bypasses ψ and the effect on uptake is version-dependent.

    Paired do() regimes: baseline versus intervened

    Both regimes share the initial state, the non-targeted forcing, the parameters, the community and the ontology. Only the target is replaced, so the difference is a counterfactual contrast for the same specified unit. Setting a target to its current value leaves the trajectory unchanged.

    Treatment versions: one total, four reference schedules, 24 parameter members

    “Add 12 mm of water” is a superclass of schedules. The comparison reports each version’s uptake in all 24 parameter members, which versions are Pareto-optimal (maximise uptake; minimise water input, residual salt and disturbed area), and whether the ranking of versions changes across members.

    External transfer tests: does the mechanism travel to other deserts?

    These are retrospective tests on independent observations. The Namib and Sonoran hydration microcosms are interventional data of the form do(θ) on bottled soil; the Sonoran field campaigns are observational. Numbers are the reviewed tables bundled with the package (/v3/transfer).

    Model evaluation scope

    Molecular forecast backtest. Campaign-blocked backtests of the ridge forecast of genomic capacity (an associational model, not the causal model) use nominal 90% empirical residual intervals; the observed temporal coverage is 85.3% across 278 held-out visits. This backtest does not evaluate any intervention. Intervention comparisons. Pareto comparisons of water-addition regimes use the 24 parameter members above and are sensitivity sets, not probabilities.

    Glossary

    Kobayashi-MaRust
    The OWL 2 reasoner (a Rust implementation) used to classify the reference MAGs. Its entailments, not table flags, decide class membership.
    M00165
    The KEGG module for the reductive pentose phosphate (Calvin–Benson–Bassham, CBB) cycle. “All 11 steps” means each step has at least one accepted KEGG ortholog, including alternative routes such as transaldolase-based regeneration.
    rak:
    Namespace prefix of the Rub’ al-Khali knowledge base, https://rubalkhali.science/kb/; kegg: names KEGG orthologs.
    MAG
    Metagenome-assembled genome. 5,438 dereplicated reference MAGs are classified.
    Genome equivalents (GE)
    Marker-gene read counts normalised by reads on single-copy core genes, so that a value of 1 corresponds to roughly one genome’s worth of the marker per cell-equivalent. A capacity measure, not activity.
    γ (community factor)
    Multiplier on a gas’s reaction coefficient derived from the entailed carrier share of the chosen community (reference, gate or proportional).
    do()
    Intervention operator: do(X = x) replaces the mechanism of X by a constant and leaves definitions, evidence and ontology untouched.
    Admissible
    A target that is manipulable (an exogenous input, or the state W or N) and for which the resulting regime is physically feasible.
    Version
    A concrete way to realise a constituted or aggregate intervention, such as a pulse schedule for a total water amount, or the matric versus osmotic realisation of ψ.
    zero_by_deduction
    The selected community has no entailed carrier of a gas, so its uptake is zero in every admissible regime. This reflects missing annotation evidence, not proven biological absence.
    θ, ψ, W, N
    Volumetric water content, total water potential (MPa), stored water (mm) and salt inventory (mol m⁻²).
    Pareto membership
    A schedule is Pareto-optimal in a member if no other schedule is at least as good on all four objectives and better on one.
    RMSE
    Root-mean-square error of held-out predictions, here in nmol H₂ m⁻² s⁻¹ unless noted.
