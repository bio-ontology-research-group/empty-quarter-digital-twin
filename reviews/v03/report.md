# Scientific and reproducibility audit for v0.4.0

This directory retains its historical name so downstream paths remain stable.
Its current outputs are generated by `reproducibility/v03/reproduce.py`.

The complete KEGG M00165 definition supports **232 CBB candidates**, including
**136 hydrogenase-plus-CBB candidates**, 100 passing the declared 90%/5% quality
screen, and 34 also carrying a form-I CO marker. The independent source-expression
parser, production table and OWL reasoner agree on every reference MAG. Of these
candidates, 149 require the transaldolase route in the absence of an annotated
sedoheptulose bisphosphatase. Genomic support does not establish activity.

Deep sand has a larger mapped CBB share than roots in 107 matched site--campaign
comparisons: mean difference 4.38 percentage points, site-bootstrap interval
[2.91, 6.00]. See `audit.json` for all contrasts and denominators. The previous
root-centred ecological interpretation is withdrawn. Archaeal taxonomy and a
Rubisco KO do not establish enzyme form or nucleoside-salvage function; two
archaeal Rubisco-positive MAGs have PRK annotations.

Resource queries read verified reasoner exports. A synthesis-eligible MAG without
a CO marker cannot use CO respiration for energy. A regression test exercises this
counterexample. Missing entailment means excluded under the evidence policy,
not demonstrated biological absence.

The simulator resolves wetting and drying within each day. The single and four-pulse
schedules reverse uptake rankings across the 24-member grid. Refinement from 96
to 384 steps preserves this reversal; maximum relative uptake difference is
below 0.1% across nonzero grid outcomes. The intact control is the only scenario
on every four-objective Pareto front.

Ridge improves on persistence across 278 future-campaign visits: pooled paired
RMSE difference −0.055 with site-bootstrap interval [−0.091, −0.019]. Its difference
from the compartment mean is −0.006 [−0.013, 0.002]. Nominal 90% empirical intervals
cover 85.3% of future visits. They are not distribution-free intervals under
campaign shift. Persistence calibration at held-out sites uses its fallback.

The 77-bottle analysis evaluates a diffusion versus activation–diffusion envelope,
with the threshold informed by the same study. It is not independent validation
of that threshold, a local kinetic calibration, or full-simulator validation.

`/v3/intervene` evaluates paired physical regimes with shared evidence and
definitions. The same-input direct calculation agrees exactly. This implements
an explicit intervention contract, not the full causal-deductive calculus or
observational do-calculus identification.

The released analysis starts at the bundled annotations, read summaries and
metadata. All source hashes are checked, the reasoner binary is pinned, and no
private checkout is used. `audit.json`, `backtest.json`, `comparison.json`,
`external_moisture.json`, `ontology_reasoning.json` and `results.json` expose the
numerical evidence. Regression tests address independent pathway parsing,
reaction admission, artifact tampering, within-day integration and intervention
semantics. A clean-checkout workflow rebuilds results and the manuscript.

Local gas-flux, retention, active-biomass and intervention measurements remain
necessary for a calibrated field twin. No automated state-assimilation loop,
prospective accuracy result or experimentally validated carbon-removal estimate
is claimed.
