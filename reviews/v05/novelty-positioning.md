# Novelty positioning and prior-art status

Working title: A causal–ontological digital twin of the world's largest continuous
sand desert.

Central contribution: connect deductively defined biological capacities and
measurement semantics to causal mechanisms in a desert twin grounded in primary
Rub' al-Khali observations, then test which predicted responses transfer to
independent deserts and environments.

## TwinDryLands

Primary source, checked 2026-10-29:
https://research.ugent.be/web/result/project/c70523a6-7391-4c60-acdd-d316b26d3cfb/details/twindrylands-12a7826-a-digital-twin-for-assessing-dryland-ecosystem-functioning-with-machine-learning-twindrylands/en

The Ghent project lists funding and a duration of 15 December 2025 to 30 April
2030. Its description presents development objectives. It is evidence of an
ongoing research programme, not evidence of a completed, published or operational
desert digital twin. Do not cite the project as defeating a first-implemented-twin
claim. Conversely, a scheduled end date alone does not establish that no interim
implementation exists; priority depends on documented outputs.

Its stated methods combine Earth observation, artificial intelligence, dynamic
vegetation models and machine-learning emulation. It also explicitly mentions
identification of causal drivers. Accordingly, avoid claiming it is purely
correlational or has no causal component. The distinctive method to establish
here is explicit ontological deduction coupled to causal intervention semantics,
with demonstrated consequences for admissibility and interpretation. The project
page does not describe such an ontology/deduction architecture.

## Priority claim

A documented implementation based on primary observations is categorically
different evidence from a funded objective. Assess completed implementations and
publications separately from proposals. Preserve the candidate claim of a first
desert digital twin while conducting that search; do not silently promote a
non-exhaustive search to proof of global priority. Geographic scope in the paper
must refer to the sampled transect and represented processes; desert identity
does not imply measurements throughout its entire area.

## Four-part argument

1. Ontological definitions determine admissible biological states and valid
   observation maps. Causal mechanisms compute consequences of specified physical
   interventions while preserving these definitions.
2. Primary desert sampling instantiates this representation, linking sites,
   specimens, sequences, genome capacities and environmental scenarios.
3. Rub' al-Khali supplies the geographical application: the world's largest
   continuous sand desert, with explicit sampling coverage.
4. Independent cross-environment tests distinguish transferable mechanisms,
   locally variable parameters and failed predictions.

The manuscript should lead with these scientific contributions. Reproducibility
and software quality support them, rather than supplying the main novelty claim.

## Completed primary-source check of the desertification preprint

10.5281/zenodo.21110603, deposited 1 July 2026, was retrieved from the Zenodo
record and its PDF read in full. It proposes a Digital Twin Desert System,
introduces a weighted resilience index and lists conceptual modules/scenarios.
The document reports an 'Expected Results' section but provides no specified
primary desert cohort, executable implementation, numerical result tables or
external validation. It is relevant conceptual prior art, not documentation of
an implemented primary-data desert microbial twin. Preserve this distinction.

Other primary sources checked: the University of Arizona's October 2026 Biosphere
2 announcement describes planned development of an AI-powered LEO landscape twin;
the University of Tarapaca May 2026 project advertisement concerns precision
agriculture in the Atacama. Destination Earth's implemented desert-locust service
uses weather/climate twins to predict breeding suitability and swarm migration.
Its documented endpoint differs from a genome-resolved microbial desert twin.

Searches on 2026-10-29 included 'desert digital twin ontology', 'desert digital twin
ecosystem implemented microbial', the exact phrase 'desert ecosystem', and named
Sahara, Atacama and Rub al-Khali queries. No examined source documents the specific
combination implemented here. This is a bounded literature search, not an exhaustive
proof of priority. A qualified first claim should specify desert microbial
function, primary genome-resolved observations, deduction and causal interventions.
