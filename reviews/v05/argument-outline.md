# Argument and evidence plan

Central contribution: A causal–ontological digital twin links primary Rub' al-Khali
observations to explicit biological definitions and intervention mechanisms, and
uses external environmental tests to locate the boundary of their transfer.

Introduction: Desert ecosystem function depends on microbial processes that are
poorly captured by vegetation-centred ecosystem representations. Primary sampling
provides genomic evidence, but a model must establish what each observation can
support before using it to predict a physical intervention. Existing annotation
and process approaches address parts of this problem. Ontological deduction
supplies constitutive constraints; causal models propagate interventions while
preserving those constraints. We instantiate their connection in the largest
continuous sand desert and evaluate the mechanisms across independent contexts.

Results progression:
1. Primary sampling instantiates a geographically explicit desert twin. Show
   field-to-genome-to-model provenance and the actual spatial/campaign coverage.
2. Deduction constrains biological interpretation and intervention computations.
   Carry one hydrogen-plus-CBB example through its evidence, reasoning, admitted
   resource calculation and physical intervention. Compare exact flat knowledge,
   missing knowledge and explicit semantic conflations. Place software verification
   in Methods and supplementary results.
3. Primary data reveal the spatial/compartment distribution of relevant capacities.
   Matched within-site/campaign contrasts motivate where process scenarios apply;
   temporal forecasts assess observation updates separately from physical flux.
4. External tests reveal which water-response mechanisms transfer. Lead with
   independent desert results, compare reference parameterization and calibration,
   retain failures, then contrast temperate environments. Measurement semantics
   determine which comparisons are scientifically admissible.
5. Conditional management scenarios expose intervention-version dependence. Link
   pulse timing and pore properties to the mechanisms tested in the preceding
   section. Present uncertainty as parameter-dependent alternatives.

Discussion: Explain what the connected representation contributes, what external
transfer reveals about desert microbial function, and which local flux observations
would reduce remaining ambiguity. Document prior art by implemented capability and
published evidence; a funded project objective is not an operational predecessor.

Evidence still needed before full prose: external numerical tests, direct specimen
and campaign inventory, a worked deductive-to-causal trace, baseline comparisons,
rendered figures and a clean independent reproduction.
